About Derby Pest Control

A local pest control service covering Derby and the surrounding area, built around fixed pricing, BPCA-standard treatment, and a follow-up when the job needs one.

Derby Pest Control treats rats, mice, squirrels, wasps, ants, cockroaches, fleas, carpet beetles, cluster flies, moths and bed bugs across Derby and a wide area of the surrounding towns and villages, for homes, landlords and businesses alike. We're a growing, Derby-focused operation, not a national franchise working from a script, and every visit is carried out to the same standard regardless of which technician attends.

How We Work

Every job starts with a proper conversation, not a guess. Tell us what you've seen, heard or found, and where, and we'll give you a fixed price on the phone before anything is booked. That price doesn't change once a technician arrives, regardless of what they find on site.

Treatment follows British Pest Control Association guidelines throughout, with bait and products placed in tamper-resistant, child and pet safe stations. Where a pest makes it necessary, rodent jobs in particular, a follow-up visit is included as standard rather than sold separately, and we'd rather identify the actual entry point than just treat the symptom and hope it doesn't come back.

Where We Cover

Derby city centre and every surrounding suburb, plus a wide area of Derbyshire and South Derbyshire beyond it, market towns like Belper, riverside villages like Shardlow, and everywhere in between.
