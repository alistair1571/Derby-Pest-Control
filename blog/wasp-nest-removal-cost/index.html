Wasp Nest Removal Cost: What to Expect

Wasp nest removal prices vary more than people expect, and the reasons usually come down to size, location and timing rather than anything hidden in the small print. Here's what actually affects the cost, and why a fixed price matters more than it sounds.

What a Typical Nest Costs to Treat

Most single wasp nest treatments in Derby run from around £65 to £90, covering a same-day or next-day visit and treatment of the nest itself. That price is usually confirmed on the phone before anyone comes out, based on where the nest is and how accessible it is, not adjusted upward once a technician arrives.

What Changes the Price

Location matters more than size for most jobs. A nest in an easily reached shed or garage is more straightforward than one deep in a loft void or under roof tiles that need careful access. Height is a factor too: a nest at ground level or in a low shed costs less to treat safely than one high up on a roofline or chimney breast requiring extra equipment.

Timing plays a role as well. Nests treated early in the season, while still small, are generally quicker jobs than a fully established nest in August holding thousands of wasps at peak activity.

Why DIY Sprays Often Cost More in the End

A can of shop-bought wasp spray looks like the cheaper option, but it frequently doesn't clear the nest, since reaching the actual nest entrance safely, especially in a loft or under eaves, is harder than it looks from the ground. A provoked but not cleared nest can mean a second attempt, wasted product, and a higher risk of stings in the meantime. Professional treatment that clears it properly in one visit is often the cheaper route once a failed DIY attempt is factored in.

Is the Nest Itself Removed?

Usually not on the same visit. The nest is treated on site, and wasps clear over the following day or two as returning wasps make contact with the treatment. Removing the physical nest too early can disturb and scatter the colony rather than let the treatment work through it properly.

Common questions

Does the price go up if the nest is bigger?

Not usually. Our pricing is based on location and access rather than nest size, since most treatments work the same way regardless of how many wasps are inside.

What if I'm not sure it's actually a wasp nest?

That's fine, describe what you've seen and where, and we can usually tell from that alone, or confirm on the visit.
