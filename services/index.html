Common questions

How do you decide what to charge for a job?

Price is based on the pest, the property and how the treatment needs to be carried out, not a flat rate applied regardless of the job. We confirm the exact price on the phone before booking, based on what you've described, and it doesn't change once the technician arrives.

Do I need to know exactly which pest I have before calling?

No. Describe what you've seen, heard or found and we'll identify it, either over the phone or on the visit. Getting the identification right matters more than guessing yourself, since some pests that look similar need genuinely different treatments.

Is every service available same-day?

Most are. Active infestations and wasp nests are prioritised within 2 to 4 hours where possible. Less urgent jobs, like a pest inspection ahead of a house purchase, are usually booked a little further out to suit your schedule.
