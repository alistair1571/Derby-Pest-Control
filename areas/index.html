Areas We Cover

Derby city centre and every surrounding suburb, plus a wide area of Derbyshire and South Derbyshire beyond it.

Pest patterns aren't the same everywhere. Older streets near Derby's city centre, many of them Victorian or interwar, see more rodent activity through ageing brickwork and original airbricks. Riverside spots like Spondon, Alvaston and Shardlow see rats pushed toward gardens after heavy rain. Newer estates like Oakwood and Stenson Fields see comparatively more wasps and insects. Common questions

What's the difference between the two lists below?

The first covers Derby itself, the city centre and its surrounding suburbs. The second covers nearby towns and villages across Derbyshire and South Derbyshire that we also reach, places like Belper, Duffield and Shardlow, which are genuinely separate settlements rather than parts of Derby.

What if my town or village isn't listed?

Call us and ask. This list covers where we have local knowledge worth writing a dedicated page for, but our coverage often extends a little further than what's listed here.

Does coverage or pricing change outside Derby itself?

Response times can be slightly longer for the more distant villages, and we'll always confirm that honestly on the phone, but pricing works the same way everywhere: confirmed upfront, based on the pest and property, not the postcode.
