About Stenson Fields

Stenson Fields is a modern residential development just southwest of Derby, built up substantially in recent decades as part of the area's expansion toward Stenson and the wider South Derbyshire border.

As with Derby's other newer estates, tighter modern construction means fewer of the small gaps and original airbricks that let rats and mice into older housing. What we see more of instead is wasps and ants, drawn to newer gardens, sheds and fencing that come with a younger housing estate.

Weather, Seasons and How They Affect Pests in Stenson Fields

Like the rest of Derby, Stenson Fields sits in a mild, wet, temperate climate, with summers that are warm without being extreme, usually peaking in the high teens to low twenties Celsius through July and August.

That kind of summer is exactly what extends the wasp and insect season in areas like Stenson Fields. Warmth and regular rain keep gardens, sheds and hedgerows growing right through the season, giving wasps, ants and other insects more time and more undisturbed spots to settle into than a shorter, harsher summer would allow. It's a different weather driven pattern to the rodent activity seen in Derby's older, denser streets, and it's why insect call outs make up more of our work here.

How to Reduce Pest Problems in Stenson Fields

A few practical, area-specific steps that genuinely help, on top of anything a professional treatment covers.

  • Check shed and garage doors for gaps each spring, before a wasp nest has the chance to establish for the season, since a small gap ignored in April is a full nest by August.
  • Keep compost bins and garden waste reasonably tidy. Undisturbed corners are exactly what ants and wasps look for when choosing where to nest.
  • Inspect eaves and soffits once a year on newer builds like those in Stenson Fields, since even tight modern construction can develop small gaps as a property settles.
