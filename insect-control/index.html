Common questions

Not sure which insect you're dealing with?

That's fine, call us or describe what you've seen and we'll identify it on the visit. Getting the pest right matters, since ants, carpet beetles and moths all need different treatments.

Do you treat more than one insect in a single visit?

Yes, if they're affecting the same property, for example ants in the kitchen and moths in a bedroom wardrobe can often be handled on the same call-out.

Are treatments safe around food, kids and pets?

Yes, we use products and application methods appropriate for each situation, and we'll always talk you through anything that needs a surface wipe-down or re-entry time before we start.
